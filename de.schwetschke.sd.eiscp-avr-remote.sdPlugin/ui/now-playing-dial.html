Left as they are, the dial changes the volume and mutes on a press.

The cover fills the whole segment and the title, artist and time are drawn over it, so a bright sleeve needs darkening to stay readable.

Album art is square and the strip is wide, so only the middle band of the sleeve fits. This shifts that band up or down when it would otherwise slice through somebody's head. On sleeves where nothing is found — black and white, drawings — the cover stays centred as before.

Swaps the track display for the volume bar or the selected input for a moment, then goes back. Only your own use of this dial triggers it — a change made on the remote control leaves the track showing.

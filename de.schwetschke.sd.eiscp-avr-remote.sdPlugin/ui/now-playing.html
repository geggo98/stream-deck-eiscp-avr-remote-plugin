The play symbol sits in the middle of the key and hides most of the artwork, so it stays out of the way while a cover is showing. It still appears when the receiver reports that playback is paused, which is the one thing a picture cannot say.

Title and artist are drawn over the cover in the Stream Deck's own font. Showing them only after a change leaves the artwork clear the rest of the time.

Shown only while the receiver reports a track length it means — some sources give none. Its colour is picked from the part of the cover it is drawn on, so it stays visible on a dark sleeve and on a bright one.

A bright album sleeve swallows white lines. Darkening it a little keeps the symbol and the title readable; turn it down for an undimmed picture.

Switching the press to the track info makes the key a display only — nothing is sent to the receiver, so a press cannot pause the music by accident.

Cover art has to be enabled on the receiver itself. It is a device-wide setting shared with every other controller on the network, so this plugin only reads it and never switches it.
